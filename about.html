How we operate

We have strong opinions about this work, and you should know them before we start.

Strategy first, AI second. We won't tell you to "adopt AI." We find the thing your business should be doing anyway, then work out how AI makes it cheaper, faster, or possible at all. Take the AI out of the plan. If there's nothing left, it wasn't a plan.

Go deep, not wide. Getting everyone using AI is the floor, and we'll help you get there. The advantage comes from one or two big moves on work that matters, finished. Twenty small wins spread across the company leave you busy and even with everyone else.

Own the result. We automate what a job is for, not the steps in it. An automation that produces reports nobody acts on has done nothing. We measure what we build against the number it was supposed to move.

The models are rented. What your business knows is yours. Every company can rent the same AI. What they can't rent is your rules, your history, and your people's judgment. We turn that into an asset your systems run on, and it gets more valuable every month.

Make your experts reach further. The best systems we build don't replace your most experienced people. They give them more reach, and they capture how those people think before it walks out the door.

Everything we build can be seen, controlled, and answered for. Every system we put into production has rules for what it may do, a record of what it did, and evidence you can hand to an auditor, an insurer, or a customer. If it can't explain itself, it doesn't ship.

Say what it is. Plain words. If we can't explain what a system does in one sentence your operations lead would use, we aren't done designing it.

Fix the cause. Fail loudly. Check before claiming. We don't call a shortcut "technical debt." We don't let a system fail quietly. And confidence isn't evidence, ours included.

You own it. Everything we build is yours: the code, the data, the controls, the plan. Handing off is as clean as staying. We stay because it's worth it, not because leaving is hard.

We run our own firm on what we sell: the books, the research, the hiring, this website.
