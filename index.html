What we do

A few of the things we build for businesses like yours.

These are examples of the kinds of things we build. Every business is different — so open the ones that sound like yours.

Institutional Knowledge
Keep and use what your people and your history know.
  • Find anything across your records, documents, and finished work in seconds.
  • Capture what your most experienced people know, before it leaves with them.
  • Bring new hires to speed in weeks, not years — and put that expertise to work as an agent your team can lean on.
Data Foundation
Get your data ready to work for you.
  • Pull scattered records and systems into one place you can actually search.
  • Turn analog files and disconnected tools into one clean, connected source of truth.
  • Build the foundation your whole business, and everything you build next, runs on.
Your best estimator has spent twenty-five years learning which jobs are worth bidding and which ones quietly bleed you dry. He retires in the spring. We capture how he thinks — the rules of thumb, the red flags, the instincts he can't quite put into words — into a system your whole team works from. The estimator who takes his chair is bidding like a veteran in her first month, and the judgment that used to live in one man's head now answers to anyone who needs it.

Surveillance
Never miss the risk or the change that matters.
  • Keep watch on what's shifting around you — regulations, markets, and the windows that move your business.
  • Monitor the health of every account and exposure, continuously — catch the customer sliding toward trouble before it costs you.
  • Build a living watch that learns your priorities and surfaces only what needs you, while there's still time to act.
Growth Intelligence
Find and win your next wave of growth.
  • Surface the upsell and cross-sell already sitting in your customer base.
  • Target the prospects that look just like your most profitable clients.
  • Expand revenue account by account — and spot the firms worth acquiring to grow faster.
You carry four hundred customers on terms, and somewhere in them two or three are quietly sliding toward trouble — paying slower, stretching their limits, leaning on you harder than they used to. Today it surfaces in a monthly report, after the exposure is already there. We build the watch that never blinks: it tracks the health of every account continuously and flags the ones turning before they cost you, while you still have room to act.

Agentic Process Automation
Put agents on the repetitive, structured work.
  • Hand off a defined, repeatable process end to end — RFPs into priced proposals, field data structured the moment crews leave the site.
  • Run it at a volume that would never be worth hiring for.
  • Free your people from the rote work for the judgment only they can bring.
Digital Workforce
More gets done, without more hires.
  • A team of agents handling the research, prep, and follow-up your people don't have time for.
  • Your best experts, with reach across far more clients, sites, and decisions.
  • Agents that own a job end to end, taking on work you could never hire for.
Every new project that comes in needs groundwork before your experts can dig in — the background pulled together, the relevant history surfaced, what matters and what's missing laid out. Nobody has the hours, so your senior people do it themselves or it gets done halfway. We give you a team of agents that owns that groundwork: for every project, they figure out what's relevant and have it worked up and waiting. Your experts walk in already briefed, and you take on more than your headcount should allow.
Stay compliant — and prove itSee how›
Compliance & Assurance
Automate the compliance work you're required to do.
  • Generate and submit mandated filings and reports straight from your operating data.
  • Track which rules apply and where you conform, across every site and job.
  • Produce the proof whenever someone asks — and know early what applies, so you can go after work you'd otherwise pass up.
Every site you run owes paperwork to somebody, and one missed filing is a fine — or a shutdown. We build the system that produces those filings from your own data, knows which rules apply where, and keeps the proof ready the moment anyone asks. Six months on, you're bidding a job two states over while your competitors are still reading the regulations to see if they're allowed.
